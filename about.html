About Me

I teach engineering by day, build ed-tech tools by evening, and spend a lot of time talking students through decisions they're anxious about.

I'm faculty at an engineering college in Maharashtra, and I've spent years watching the same handful of decisions cause the most stress every year — core vs. IT, GATE vs. placements, which skill to actually invest time in. Most of what's written about these online is either generic or trying to sell something.

Outside the classroom, I build technology products aimed at solving these exact problems at scale — but this site isn't about that. It's just me, writing and talking through the questions I get asked in my office hours, one on one.

If a guide or a session here saves you from a decision you'd have regretted, it did its job.
